STOCK GROUPS REFERENCE

Purpose: Define the exact stock_group labels used in ticker_profiles.json.
Canonical group name: The value after 'Group:' is the exact label, including emoji, that appears in ticker_profiles.json.
How to read entries: Each block between --- dividers is one independent group definition.
Meaning field: Describes what it generally means for a ticker/company to belong to that group.
Important: A company can fit a group because of its main business, revenue exposure, assets, technology, or investment theme. Not every company will match every phrase in the definition.
Important: Only records after RECORDS BEGIN are actual group definitions.
